🎯 Coach Tip of the Week After a losing game, most coaches say: "That wasn't good enough." Try this instead: "What did you do well today — even though we lost?" "What was the hardest moment?" "What will you do differently next week?" Three questions. Five minutes. And you turn a loss into a learning moment. That's not soft. That's smart coaching. Science calls this a mastery motivational climate — and it demonstrably leads to more resilience, more intrinsic motivation, and better long-term performance. Save this for your next match. 📌 #characterfirst #coachinglife #sportspsychology #youthsport #mentalperformance #evidencebased #mindset #athletedevelopment

Week 3 — The Problem with Performance-Only Focus

Science Fact
LinkedIn Tue · 08:00
📊 Het probleem met winnen als enige doel Studie: Smoll & Smith (2006) onderzocht ruim 500 jeugdcoaches. Resultaat: Coaches die uitsluitend op resultaat focusten, hadden spelers met: → Hogere angst voor falen → Lagere sportcontinuïteit (meer uitval) → Minder teamcohesie Coaches die bewust werkten aan mastery climate hadden het omgekeerde effect. Het gaat niet om winnen of verliezen. Het gaat om hoe je wint en verliest. Character First is gebouwd op deze evidentie. Geen motivatiespeech. Geen plakkaat met clichés. Concrete oefeningen die bewijs hebben. #characterfirst #sportpsychologie #jeugdsport #coachontwikkeling #evidencebased #mentalekracht #sporteducatie
Visual Description

LinkedIn-style infographic. Two columns: "Performance-only" vs "Mastery climate" with bullet outcomes. Orange/dark color scheme. Study citation at bottom in small text.

📊 The problem with winning as the only goal Study: Smoll & Smith (2006) researched over 500 youth coaches. Finding: Coaches focused solely on results had players with: → Higher fear of failure → Lower sport continuation (more dropout) → Less team cohesion Coaches who deliberately worked on mastery climate had the opposite effect. It's not about winning or losing. It's about how you win and how you lose. Character First is built on this evidence. No motivational speech. No cliché poster. Concrete exercises with a scientific basis. #characterfirst #sportspsychology #youthsport #coachinglife #evidencebased #mentalperformance #athletedevelopment
