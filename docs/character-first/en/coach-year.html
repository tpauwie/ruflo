The program

Month by month

Months 1 and 2 are freely accessible as an introduction. Months 3 to 10 are part of the full Coach Year track.

1
Month 1 — Free preview

Who am I as a coach?

"What are my deepest values as a coach, and how are they visible in my behaviour?"

  • Coach identity exercise: three words that define you as a coach
  • Write your personal sport history — how has your own sporting past shaped your coaching?
  • Fill in the values compass and build a bridge to daily coaching decisions
Scientific foundation: Cushion et al., 2010 — Coach knowledge & identity formation

Month 2 — Free preview

My relationship with players

"How do I build genuine trust with every individual in my group?"

  • Attachment theory in sport: how attachment shapes the coach-athlete bond
  • Set up an individual check-in protocol for each athlete
  • Conduct a psychological safety audit of your team
Scientific foundation: Jowett & Ntoumanis, 2004 — The coach-athlete relationship

Motivational Climate

"What climate do I create consciously or unconsciously, and how can I change it?"

  • Mastery vs. performance climate self-scan and observation log

Communication

"When do I ask questions and when do I tell? How do I build a feedback culture?"

  • Analyse instruction techniques through video observation of your own sessions
  • Question-based coaching: 30-day challenge (asking questions instead of telling)
  • Design a feedback protocol for your group
Scientific foundation: Turnnidge & Côté, 2018 — Coach communication

Dealing with parents

  • Map the parent-coach-athlete triangle for your group

Positive Youth Development

"Do I see the whole person, or only the athlete? How do I align my expectations with the development stage?"

Reflection as a growth tool
