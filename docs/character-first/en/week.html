5 Days of Character

An intensive camp program for young athletes who want to become more than just a good athlete.

The program

Day by day

1
Day one
Self-Knowledge
"Who am I as an athlete?"
  • Sport timeline: visualise your journey from first memory to now
  • Values auction: discover your top 3 core values in sport
  • Identity anchors: articulate who you are as an athlete
Morning Afternoon Evening
2
Day two
Pressure & Stress
"How do I react under pressure?"
  • Box Breathing and 4-7-8 breathing techniques in action
  • Stress ladder: map your personal pressure points
  • Pressure scenarios: practise responses to high-pressure situations
Morning Afternoon Evening
3
Day three
My Role
"What is my contribution to the team?"
  • Role compass: map formal and informal roles
  • Human Graph: explore team positions and diversity
  • Role pledge: public commitment about your team behaviour
Morning Afternoon Evening
4
Day four
Communication
"How do I speak and listen?"
  • Mirror Conversation: active listening without interrupting
  • Feedback Round Trio: giving and receiving in groups of three
  • Encouragement challenge: specific and spontaneous encouragement
Morning Afternoon Evening
5
Day five
Team First
"What does it mean to choose for each other?"
  • Trust fall: building physical trust in pairs
  • Team contract: establishing shared behavioural norms
  • Closing ceremony: three circle rounds — word, lesson, wish
Morning Afternoon Evening
