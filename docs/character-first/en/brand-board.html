01 — Brand Core

What the brand stands for

Every design choice in this book — colour, shape, language — originates from this core. The brand lives at the intersection of two forces: the fire of the individual and the steadiness of growth and character.

Mission

Help every young athlete grow as a person, not only as a competitor.

Sport is the perfect stage for developing life values. We give coaches and clubs an evidence-based framework to train mental resilience, teamwork, self-knowledge and leadership — alongside technique and tactics.

Vision

A generation that wins on the field and in life.

We see a future where character is trained as systematically as technique. Where coaches are judged by the people they build — and where Character First is the standard, not the exception.

Don't

  • Set the CF letters in any typeface other than Archivo.
  • Place the orange mountain on a red or busy background without an outline.
03 — Colour

Colour palette & rationale

The previous palette leaned heavily on near-black (#0a0a0a) and felt dark and closed. The new palette retains the recognisable orange, replaces hard black with a warmer navy, and builds on a light, warm base — inviting and youthful, fitting for athletes from age 14. Every colour carries meaning from the mission.

06 — Tone

Tone of voice & imagery

The voice follows the values: courageous, growth-minded, evidence-based and together.

How we sound

Do: direct, warm and activating. Short sentences. "You" and "we". A coach who stands beside you, not above you.

Don't: vague, preachy or over-the-top motivational quotes. No empty hype without evidence.

How we look

Do: real young athletes in action and at rest, natural light, genuine emotion. Lots of white space on canvas. Orange as an accent.

Don't: stock-photo clichés, heavy dark backgrounds throughout, or imagery that is only about winning.
