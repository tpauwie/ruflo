The story

CF is the foundation. The mountain is where you are going.

The mountain represents ambition — the summit every athlete and coach strives towards. The letters CF form the foundation the mountain rests on: character above all else. The white snow peak is the moment of arrival, but also the start of the next climb. Character First builds the person who can reach the summit — not just the performance at the top.

Tone of voice

How we sound

Direct, evidence-based, warm but not soft. We speak to coaches and young athletes as capable people.

Do

Concrete and grounded. "60–70% of mental resilience is trainable." Numbers, sources, applicable at training.

Don't

Vague or soft. No empty motivational quotes without evidence. No jargon without explanation.

Do

People-focused. "Win the person. Win the team." We build people, not just performance.

Don't

Performance above all. Never winning at the cost of the athlete's growth.
