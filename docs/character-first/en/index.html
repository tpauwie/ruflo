Who we are

Mission & Vision

Mission

Help every young athlete grow as a person, not just as an athlete.

Character First believes sport is the perfect stage for developing life values. We give coaches and clubs an evidence-based framework to train mental resilience, team spirit, self-knowledge, and leadership alongside technique.

Vision

A generation that wins on the field and in life.

We envision a future where character is trained as systematically as technique — where coaches are judged by the people they develop, and where Character First is the standard, not the exception.

Our logo

CF is the foundation. The mountain is where you are going.

The mountain represents ambition — the summit every athlete and coach strives towards. The letters CF form the foundation the mountain rests on: character above all else. The white snow peak is the moment of arrival, but also the start of the next climb. Character First builds the person who can reach the summit — not just the performance at the top.
