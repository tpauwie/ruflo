Why now

What is at stake

Technical training is everywhere. Mental and character development much less so.

"35% of young athletes quit before the age of 15. The main reason: they feel they don't belong."

Visek et al., 2015 — George Washington University

"Athletes with high mental resilience perform up to 40% more consistently under pressure than athletes with low resilience."

Gucciardi et al., 2015 — Journal of Sport and Exercise Psychology

"Coaches spend on average less than 2% of training time on mental skills, while 90% acknowledge it is equally important."

Weinberg & Gould, 2019 — Foundations of Sport Psychology

"Teams with high psychological safety perform 26% better than teams with low psychological safety."

Edmondson, 2018 — Harvard Business School

Evidence-based

Grounded in science

All exercises are based on peer-reviewed literature from sports psychology, organisational psychology and education.

Systematic Review
Gucciardi et al., 2015

Mental Toughness in Sport

Mental resilience is 60 to 70% trainable through targeted intervention. Meta-analysis of 91 studies.

RCT Study
Hatzigeorgiadis et al., 2009

Self-Talk and Performance

Athletes who received self-talk training performed 17% better under high pressure than the control group.

Systematic Review
Fletcher & Sarkar, 2012

Resilience in Elite Sport

Resilience in athletes is strongly associated with positive personality, motivation and social support.

Qualitative
Visek et al., 2015

Fun Integration Theory

Belonging and having fun are the strongest predictors of sports participation in young people up to 15 years old.

RCT Study
Duckworth et al., 2007

Grit: Perseverance and Passion

Perseverance and passion are stronger predictors of success than intelligence or talent alone.

Systematic Review
Edmondson, 2018

Psychological Safety

Teams with high psychological safety innovate more, make fewer mistakes and perform 26% better.
