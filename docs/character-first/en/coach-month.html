Coach Month — 8 Contact Sessions per Season

For coaches who want to grow but have little time. One powerful 90-minute session per month — practical, directly applicable, with immediate impact on your training.

Session Format 8 Sessions Sign Up
How it works

Every session, the same rhythm

Consistency makes growth possible. Every contact session follows the same structure so you always know what to expect — and what is asked of you.

90 min

Fixed session structure — every month

15 min

Check-in & Reflection

Looking back on the previous month. What did you try? What worked? What was difficult? No judgement, only observation and learning.

45 min

Core Theme of the Month

A combination of theory (compact and practical) and exercise. Every coach participates actively — no passive listening.

20 min

Concrete Application

You translate the theme into your own training plan for the coming month. What will you specifically do differently? When? With whom?

10 min

Personal Action

One concrete, measurable action for the coming month. You write it down. We return to it in the next session.
